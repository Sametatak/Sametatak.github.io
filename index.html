About

As a senior Control and Automation Engineering student at Istanbul Technical University (ITU), I have focused my work on autonomous robotic systems and machine learning. Through academic research at the University of Kassel (Germany) and industrial projects at Machine Arge, I gained experience in transforming theoretical foundations into functional field systems. I demonstrated my competence in ROS, embedded systems, and deep learning by securing 1st place in the international NJORD competition. I aim to contribute value as an engineer to innovative projects developing autonomous technologies.

MPPI ROS2 Navigation Planner

A ROS2 navigation prototype built around a Python MPPI controller. It publishes sampled rollouts, the selected trajectory, costmap data and target markers so the behavior can be inspected in RViz while Gazebo runs the scene.

  • Diff-drive kinematic model with sampled velocity commands.
  • Cost function balancing target distance, heading error and obstacle cost.
  • Simple global planner and pure-pursuit lookahead target selection.
  • Launch flow for costmap, global planner, MPPI controller and RViz.
ros2 launch my_mpc_planner start_world.launch.py
ros2 launch my_mpc_planner navigation2_mppi.launch.py
ros2 launch my_mpc_planner mppi_launch.py
